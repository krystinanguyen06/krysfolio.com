About

Hello, I'm Krystina, a visual designer and animator based in Melbourne.

I'm studying Graphic Design & User Experience Design at the University of Melbourne. Over the past three years, I've turned clients' ideas into visual experiences through creative packaging and animation. I specialise in 3D packaging design and hand-drawn 2D animation, and I believe every detail should have a purpose.

I'd love to hear about your vision and work out how we can bring it to life together.
