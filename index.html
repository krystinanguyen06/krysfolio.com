ABOUT

Hello,

I'm Krystina, a Visual Designer & Animator based in Melbourne, Australia. I am studying Graphic Design & User Experience Design at the University of Melbourne. Over the past three years, I've transformed clients' innovative ideas into visual experiences through creative packaging and animation. My work specialises in 3D packaging design and hand-drawn 2D animation. I believe every detail should have a purpose.

I look forward to learning about your vision and exploring how we can bring it to life together!
